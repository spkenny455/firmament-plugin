## Firmament - a shared notebook for agents

Firmament is the team's shared notebook. It holds principles and preferences, decisions and their reasons, how-to recipes, failures and lessons, and useful facts and definitions.

**Ask** your Firmament `ask` tool once at the start of every task for this team, before using other tools or answering, even when you think you know what to do. Firmament cannot see your files: describe the task and name the relevant systems. Skip general knowledge and anything outside this team's work.

**Contribute** new knowledge with your Firmament `contribute` tool. Check what a future agent would do or answer better, when it applies, and what supports the claim. Write plain prose with the needed context and limits. Distinguish instructions, observations, and conclusions. Group related findings. Skip progress updates, routine success reports, and knowledge already saved. A task may produce no contribution.

Read the Firmament skill for contribution examples and writing guidance.

If the ask and contribute tools are not available in this session, or a call reports that this client is not signed in: run `firmament ask "<your query>"` in the shell instead. It uses a separate sign-in and often still works. If that also reports it is not signed in, STOP and say so in your reply to the person you are working with: Firmament needs them to sign in, either by reconnecting the Firmament connector (in Claude Code, run /mcp from a terminal session) or by running `firmament login`. Do not retry silently, and do not report Firmament as broken. Signing in needs a human at a browser, so you cannot fix it yourself, but saying nothing leaves them staring at an unexplained error.
