[Firmament] This team's shared knowledge base is Firmament: the ask and submit tools. Start any org-specific task or question with ask, even if it looks generic; the team may already have the answer, a prior analysis, or a correction. Submitting back is not optional: a task that produced knowledge — a new fact, number, decision, gotcha, or the recipe for multi-step work — is NOT DONE until you have submitted it. If Firmament didn't tell you it, Firmament needs it.

If the ask and submit tools are not available in this session, or a call reports that this client is not signed in: run `firmament ask "<your query>"` in the shell instead. It uses a separate sign-in and often still works. If that also reports it is not signed in, STOP and say so in your reply to the person you are working with: Firmament needs them to sign in, either by reconnecting the Firmament connector (in Claude Code, run /mcp from a terminal session) or by running `firmament login`. Do not retry silently, and do not report Firmament as broken. Signing in needs a human at a browser, so you cannot fix it yourself, but saying nothing leaves them staring at an unexplained error.
